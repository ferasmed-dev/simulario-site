How it works

Learn by doing

Every simulator lets you change something and see what happens. You decide what to touch.

01

Explore

Go through the model layer by layer: the slices of a scan, the levels of a system, the stages of a process.

02

Change

Move a parameter. Raise the blood pressure, step to the next slice, open another case.

03

Observe

See what happens in real time and understand why. What you have watched change stays with you.
